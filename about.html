ABOUT ME

Built by a creator, for creators.

Hi, I'm Victoria, a media growth specialist helping creators and artists turn their content into a real, sustainable business.

I work at the intersection of platform strategy and creative craft. On the YouTube side, I handle everything from channel management, SEO-driven growth, and monetization strategy to hands-on video editing that keeps viewers watching. On Twitch, I help creators build the kind of consistent, engaged audience that turns casual viewers into loyal communities.

For artists, I go beyond the numbers, writing songs, running press campaigns, and promoting music to the right audiences so the work actually gets heard.

Every creator's path looks different, which is why I don't believe in one-size-fits-all growth. Whether you're just starting out or trying to break through a plateau, I build a strategy around where you are and where you want to go. Then I execute it, from the content itself to the systems behind it.

If you're ready to stop guessing and start growing, let's talk.
